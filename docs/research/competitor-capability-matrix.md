# Competitor Capability Matrix — suites, capabilities, features & benefits

A structured breakdown of how the competitors organize their products. Built from the
[competitor research](market-and-competitors.md). Pairs with the [Table Stakes](table-stakes.md)
(who-has-what) and our own [product map](../20-ecosystem-and-self-improvement.md).

**The four terms (kept straight):**
| Term | Means | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Suite / Module** (product) | a grouping of capabilities sold/branded together | "Dispatch", "Marketing Pro" |
| **Capability** | *what* a suite does | scheduling, estimating, payments |
| **Feature** | a specific function within a capability | drag-and-drop board, good/better/best options |
| **Benefit** | the *outcome/value* marketed | "send proposals in hours not days" |

> Design from **capabilities** (the build backlog); sell on **benefits** (the strategy). A feature
> list is not a value proposition.

## Adjacent — the "platform with modules" structure (horizontal, not FSM)

These aren't FSM, but they model the **suite → capabilities** shape (and the all-in-one pitch):

| Player | Suites / Modules | The shape lesson |
|---|---|---|
| **PMSuite / Zoho One / Odoo / Bitrix24** | HR & People · CRM & Sales · Finance · Projects · Workspace (each "5 tools") | clean Platform → Products → Capabilities; "replace your whole stack" — but **broad & shallow**, no field depth |
| **Infinity / ClickUp / Notion / Monday** | one dataset, many **views** (Table · Kanban · List · Calendar · Gantt · Form) | flexible views — but **you build the structure yourself** |
| **Hubstaff** | Time tracking · GPS monitoring · payroll · screenshots | the **surveillance** archetype — what *not* to be |
