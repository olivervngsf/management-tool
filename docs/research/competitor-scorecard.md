## How to read it

- **ServiceTitan** is a wall of strength on the right kind of capabilities (dispatch, depth) and a
  wall of weakness on the human ones (ease, onboarding, price, mobile). Powerful, painful.
- **Jobber / Housecall Pro** are the mirror image — easy and fast to adopt, but they thin out on
  depth and (Housecall especially) on transparent pricing.
- **Swivl** competes on price and AI for the smallest shops, but is light on the office/depth side.
- **Fieldwork's target shape:** strong *everywhere the others are weak* (mobile, offline, ease,
  onboarding, price, communication, flexibility), and **good-enough — not maximal — on office depth
  and AI**. We deliberately don't try to out-depth ServiceTitan on day one; we win the daily loop.

> The pattern to notice: **no competitor is strong in the top six rows at once.** Those six rows are
> your four goals. That cluster of weakness *is* the opportunity.

---

## What this tells us to do

1. **Don't fight on depth first.** ServiceTitan owns it; chasing it is how you become ServiceTitan.
2. **Win the top six rows** — they're under-served by *everyone* and they're your goals.
3. **Be "good enough" on dispatch depth and AI** so a growing shop never has a reason to leave.
4. **Use price + onboarding as weapons**, because the whole category is hated for the opposite.
