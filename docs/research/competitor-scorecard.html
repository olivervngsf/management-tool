The takeaway: No competitor is strong across the top six rows at once. ServiceTitan owns depth but is painful to live with; Jobber & Housecall are easy but thin out at scale; Swivl is cheap but light on the office side. Fieldwork's play: be strong everywhere they're weak (mobile, offline, ease, onboarding, price, communication, flexibility) and good-enough — not maximal on office depth & AI. Win the technician's daily loop first; don't try to out-depth ServiceTitan on day one.
